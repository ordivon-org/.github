# Contributing to Ordivon

Ordivon accepts contributions under the license of the repository receiving the contribution. The default software license is Apache-2.0.

## Developer Certificate of Origin

Contributions use the Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) 1.1 process rather than a blanket copyright assignment or heavy contributor licence agreement unless a repository explicitly documents a different requirement.

By adding a `Signed-off-by:` trailer, you certify that you have the right to submit the contribution under the repository's licence and the DCO. Use:

```text
git commit -s
```

The canonical DCO text is maintained at https://developercertificate.org/.

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions unless it is separately assigned by an explicit agreement. The repository licence grant remains effective according to its terms.

## Rights and provenance

Do not submit material you do not have the right to publish. Identify third-party code, data, media, models, or documentation and preserve their licence/attribution requirements. Do not silently copy proprietary or access-restricted material into an Ordivon repository.

## Privacy and secrets

Never commit live credentials, tokens, private keys, cookies, authentication material, raw or linkable personal records, private correspondence, or unnecessary operational identifiers. Use synthetic fixtures, aggregate evidence, redacted projections, and non-secret references instead.

## Research and security

Research claims must preserve source/provenance boundaries. Security experiments must stay inside systems Ordivon owns or is explicitly authorized to test. Vulnerability details under responsible disclosure remain private until disclosure is admitted.

See `PUBLICATION_POLICY.md` and `SECURITY.md` in this organization policy repository and any stricter repository-local policy.
